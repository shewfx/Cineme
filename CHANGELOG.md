# Changelog

## 1.2.0

Onboarding and movie discovery (below, formerly planned as 1.1.0) ship together with shows and anime in this release.

- Shows and anime: add series to the watchlist, keep your progress (“Last watched: Season 1, Episode 4”), and let Tonight recommend either one film or one next episode.
- “What to watch” (Movies only, Movies & shows, Shows only) is saved on your account; existing users stay on Movies only.
- A Watchlist filter (All, Movies only, Shows only), a Movies | Shows control on the add screen, show details with Mark watched and Set my progress, an Episodes tab in History, and Blocked shows in Profile.
- Tonight favours continuing a show you are actively watching, with a bounded bonus that fades after three idle weeks. A show's own recent viewings are exempt from the variety rule only inside that same 21-day window.
- Specials and alternate episode orders are not included yet.
- Show details now share Movie Details' blurred poster background and show “Where to watch” (your region, the show as a whole, never a promise about a particular episode); Tonight's episode card shows it too.
- Watchlist → + → Shows opens with “Trending shows this week” (12 posters, Add/Added, anime included) until you type; search results and trending share one Added state.
- Tonight's “Not this one?” sheet uses a single “Reason” dropdown (choosing never submits; “Show another” does; “Stop for tonight” needs no reason).

## 1.1.0 (not released separately; shipped inside 1.2.0)

- New accounts get a short welcome and can add films to their watchlist before their first Tonight. Skip and Continue are always available and nothing is required.
- The add-movies step opens with “Trending this week”, a short grid of posters to add from, alongside search.
- Watchlist → + offers the same discovery with “Popular releases this month” and “this year” (films released so far, by current popularity) next to search.
- Onboarding completion is stored on the server, so it resumes across devices; existing accounts are not shown it.
