# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- New accounts get a short welcome and can add films to their watchlist before their first Tonight. Skip and Continue are always available and nothing is required.
- The add-movies step opens with “Trending this week”, a short grid of posters to add from, alongside search.
- Watchlist → + offers the same discovery with “Popular releases this month” and “this year” (films released so far, by current popularity) next to search.
- Onboarding completion is stored on the server, so it resumes across devices; existing accounts are not shown it.
